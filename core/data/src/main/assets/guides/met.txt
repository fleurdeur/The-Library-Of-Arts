THE MET — A SHORT FLOOR GUIDE
Entrance: Great Hall, Egyptian Art (Gallery 100s) to the left.
European Paintings: Galleries 600–644, second floor. Start with Rembrandt (616) and Vermeer (644).
Modern and Contemporary: Galleries 900s. The rooftop garden is seasonal.
Arms and Armor: Gallery 371. American Wing: Galleries 700s.
Tip: pick three rooms, not thirty. Sit down once per visit.
