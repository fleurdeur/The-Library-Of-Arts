RIJKSMUSEUM — A SHORT FLOOR GUIDE
Gallery of Honour (Eregalerij): The Night Watch at the far end, Vermeer's Milkmaid nearby.
Floor 0: Dutch Golden Age, ship models, Delftware.
Floor 1: 18th–20th century, including Van Gogh and Mondrian.
Floor 2: Rijksprentenkabinet (prints and drawings), Library.
Tip: the garden is free; use it as a quiet reset between galleries.
