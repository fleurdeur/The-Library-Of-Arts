CLEVELAND MUSEUM OF ART — A SHORT FLOOR GUIDE
Gallery 220: Rodin's Thinker. Gallery 222: Monet's Water Lilies (Agapanthus).
Gallery 100: Asian art, including Chinese and Japanese galleries.
Gallery 200s: European art; Gallery 300s: African and American art.
Tip: admission is free; the atrium is a good place to pause.
